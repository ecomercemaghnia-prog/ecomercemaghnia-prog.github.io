Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

VIDEO N8N ("we", "our service") is an automation tool operated by ECOMERCE MAGHNIA that creates and publishes short videos to social media platforms (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube) on behalf of the account owner.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share personal data of end users. The service accesses only the social media accounts explicitly connected and authorized by the account owner via official OAuth 2.0 authorization flows.

2. How We Use Information

Access tokens granted through OAuth are used solely to upload and publish video content that the account owner has created or approved. Tokens are never used for any other purpose.

3. Data Storage

Tokens and configuration are stored in the operator's private Google Sheets and private n8n instance. Video files are stored in the operator's private Google Drive. No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The service uses the official APIs of TikTok, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google (YouTube, Drive, Sheets), and the n8n platform. Each platform's own privacy policy applies to data processed by that platform.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

The account owner may revoke access at any time through the connected platform's security settings. Once access is revoked, no further actions are performed. To request deletion of stored data, contact us at the email below.
